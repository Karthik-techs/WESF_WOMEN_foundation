Who We Are

Inspiring Independence and Leadership Since Day One

The Women's Empowerment and Sports Foundation (WESF) is dedicated to nurturing the inherent potential of Indian women and girls. We work closely with grassroots communities in rural and semi-urban India to construct environments where women are equipped, educated, and emboldened to lead. By combining athletic training with vocational, economic, and digital education, we support holistic growth and foster gender equity.

To uplift women and girls from marginalized socio-economic backgrounds by providing equal access to sports scholarships, career counseling, computer skills, health coaching, and startup guidance.

An inclusive India where every girl student is literate, every mother is financially independent, every female athlete has the resources to represent the country, and women walk as confident leaders in their communities.

Integrity: Upholding transparency and community trust.
Empathy: Understanding and addressing localized needs.
Resilience: Encouraging determination through athletic and economic pursuits.

Our Foundation Pillars

We deploy targeted solutions to foster structural changes for women across Telangana and surrounding regions.

Education & Digital Literacy

We provide schools with learning materials, run post-classroom tutoring centers, and operate digital learning hubs to train rural schoolgirls in coding, computational thinking, and software literacy.

  • Digital labs in Hyderabad & Rural sectors
  • Free scholarship drives for high school girls
  • English speaking and communications training

Sports Development

We leverage sports to foster leadership, confidence, health, and team spirit. We discover athletic potential, run sports camps, and sponsor national representations.

  • Professional training in football, archery, athletics
  • Nutritional counseling and sports kits provision
  • District and national athletic championships paths

Entrepreneurship

We empower women to become local business owners. Through vocational skill training, financial literacy workshops, and micro-loan matching, we construct avenues for economic independence.

  • Tailoring, handicrafts, and agro-business courses
  • Banking, micro-savings, and capital access tutoring
  • Marketing guidance for village-grown products

Success Stories

Real transformation accounts from our beneficiaries whose courage inspires us every day.

"WESF provided me with custom archery equipment and matched me with a professional coach in Hyderabad. Today, I hold a state gold medal, and I am preparing for national trials. WESF didn't just give me resources; they gave me a vision."

Priya Sharma

State Archer & WESF Scholar

"Before attending WESF's vocational training and banking seminars, I relied entirely on seasonal daily wage work. Now, I run my tailoring boutique. I employ two other young women and save for my daughter's secondary school fees."

Ananya Patel

Founder, Pragati Boutique Craft

"Our village high school lacked basic digital access. WESF installed modern laptops and hosted coding seminars. My students now view computing as an exciting avenue rather than an unreachable dream."

Deepa Reddy

Primary Teacher, Ramanthapur District
